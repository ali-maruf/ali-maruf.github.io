Atlas.

A calm, monochrome personal life OS. The moment you open it, it answers four questions: what's today, what can't slip, where am I, and where am I going.

Visit atlas-life-os-rho.vercel.app ↗

Problem

Tasks, classes, goals, notes and people usually live in five different apps.

None of them tells you, at a glance, what actually matters today.

What I built

  • Today view with a Top 3, a timeline, quick capture, and a "don't let slip" list.
  • Natural-language inbox, tasks on lists and boards, and a calendar where you drag tasks into time blocks.
  • Studies, projects, people and goals in one place, with a guided weekly review on Sundays.
  • A block editor for notes, Excalidraw whiteboards, a ⌘K palette, and keyboard shortcuts everywhere.
  • Installable PWA. It runs entirely on free tiers.
