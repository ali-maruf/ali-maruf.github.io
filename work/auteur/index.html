Auteur.

You talk, Auteur directs. Record your screen, face and voice, and get back an edited video with smart zoom, webcam layouts and captions. Nothing leaves your browser.

Visit auteur-pi-three.vercel.app ↗

Problem

Recording a tutorial takes ten minutes, and editing it takes an afternoon.

That afternoon goes on scrubbing for the moments that matter, zooming on every click by hand, cutting dead air and typing out captions.

What I built

  • A director that scores every second for movement, clicks, typing and speech, then picks the layout a human editor would.
  • Whisper transcription in the browser on your GPU, in English and Bangla, with word-level timing.
  • Smooth cursor-following zoom, a webcam bubble, layouts that morph between each other, and automatic dead-air removal.
  • Burned-in captions in 8 styles, and background music that ducks under your voice.
  • Private by design. Recording, analysis and rendering all run locally on WebCodecs, WebGPU and Web Workers.

Stack

TypeScript · WebCodecs · WebGPU · Web Workers · Whisper · Claude API (optional)
