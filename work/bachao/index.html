2026 — Building

Bachao.

Save good food and pay less. A surplus-food marketplace for Dhaka, where bakeries, sweet shops and restaurants sell unsold end-of-day food as discounted surprise bags.

Prototype — not public yet

Problem

Good food gets thrown away every night in Dhaka, while plenty of people would happily buy it at a discount.

Shops have no simple way to sell it in the last hour before closing.

What I built

  • A full MVP design spec covering brand directions, information architecture, flows, the design system, and the riskiest assumptions to test first.
  • A bilingual (Bangla and English) prototype with no dependencies. It covers discovery by list and map, bag details, checkout with OTP, and pickup tickets.
  • Empty, offline and payment-failed states designed from the start, not left for later.

Stack

HTML · CSS design tokens · vanilla JS · i18n
