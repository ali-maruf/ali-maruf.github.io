DhakaHype.

Things to do in Dhaka. A mobile-first city guide to concerts, melas, screenings, comedy, sports and workshops, for tonight and this weekend.

Visit dhakahype.com ↗

Problem

Events in Dhaka are scattered across Facebook pages, group chats and posters.

There was no one place to see what's happening tonight, or how long it would take to get there.

What I built

  • Tonight, This weekend and Free strips, a filterable calendar, and a date map with one pin per venue.
  • Jam-o-meter, which estimates travel time and fare by bike, CNG, rickshaw or metro, tuned to Dhaka rush hours.
  • Scraper bots with Claude-powered event extraction and duplicate detection.
  • Calendar subscriptions, a public JSON API, weather warnings for outdoor events, and an installable PWA.
  • A weekend that runs from Thursday 5 pm, because that's when Dhaka goes out.
