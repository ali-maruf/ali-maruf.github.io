DigiBikri.

The digital product marketplace for Bangladesh. Creators sell notes, ebooks, templates and courses. Buyers pay in bKash and receive their files in seconds.

Launching soon at digibikri.com

Problem

Bangladeshi creators already sell PDFs, Canva packs and video courses, mostly through Facebook inboxes and screenshots of bKash payments.

That doesn't scale, the files leak, and the buyer is left with nothing to show for the money.

What I built

  • Local payments: bKash Tokenized Checkout, Nagad, and cards through SSLCommerz, all priced in ৳.
  • Delivery that's verified on the server, so files land in the buyer's library within seconds.
  • Piracy protection: watermarks on every buyer's PDFs, signed 15-minute download links, streamed video and device limits.
  • An append-only ledger with a 7-day hold, payouts to bKash, and a full admin audit trail.
  • Bangla-first interface, plus affiliates, coupons and pay-what-you-want pricing.

Stack

PHP 8.2 · hand-written MVC · MySQL 8 · vanilla JS · no framework
